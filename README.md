# Rueda de Birria Sales Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview

**Rueda de Birria (RDB)** was a food business that I worked on with my team, where I served as the **Officer in Charge of Finance**.

As part of my role, I handled and monitored the business's financial and sales information. I created this interactive Power BI dashboard to organize our sales data and make it easier to evaluate the business's sales performance.

I worked with sales data from August 2025 to April 2026 and analyzed revenue, order volume, product performance, selling periods, and bazaar performance. I used Microsoft Excel and SQL to prepare and analyze the data, then developed the Power BI dashboard to visualize the results.

## What I Wanted to Analyze

As the Officer in Charge of Finance, I wanted to have a clearer view of how RDB was performing across different selling events and periods.

I focused on:

* Overall revenue and sales performance
* Total order volume
* Best-performing product categories
* Daily and hourly ordering patterns
* Revenue and order volume across bazaars
* Average order value across selling events
* Top-performing selling periods

## Tools Used

* **Microsoft Excel** – Data preparation and organization
* **SQL** – Data querying and analysis
* **Power BI** – Data visualization and dashboard development

## Dashboard

### Page 1 — Sales Overview

I created the Sales Overview page to provide a high-level view of RDB's sales performance. It includes key performance indicators along with daily and hourly ordering patterns.

![Sales Overview Dashboard](dashboard_page1.png)

**Key Metrics:**

* **Total Revenue:** ₱461.93K
* **Average Order Value:** ₱255.21
* **Total Birrias Sold:** 4,252
* **Total Orders:** 1,810
* **Average Birria per Order:** 2.35

**What I Found:**

* Fridays recorded the highest number of orders.
* Most orders were placed between **11 AM and 5 PM**.
* The **Mains** category generated the majority of sales, accounting for approximately **92.9%** of total sales.

### Page 2 — Bazaar Performance

I created the Bazaar Performance page to compare revenue, order volume, and average order value across different selling events and channels.

![Bazaar Performance Dashboard](dashboard_page2.png)

**What I Found:**

* **UST SIBOL** generated the highest revenue at approximately **₱76.03K**.
* UST SIBOL also recorded the highest number of orders with **366 orders**.
* **Pre-Order** generated approximately **₱55.24K** in revenue.
* **UST Tunog Tomasino** generated approximately **₱54.24K** in revenue.
* **Birthday Bulk Order** had the highest average order value at **₱20,000**, although this was based on a single order.

## Business Insights

From the analysis, I found that RDB's sales performance was strongly influenced by **selling events, time of day, and product category**.

The higher order activity on Fridays and between **11 AM and 5 PM** highlighted the periods when the business experienced greater customer activity. This information can be useful when considering inventory preparation and staffing.

The **Mains** category accounted for the majority of sales, showing that RDB's core products were the primary contributors to revenue.

The Bazaar Performance analysis also allowed me to compare our different selling events and identify which events generated higher revenue and order volume. This can help when evaluating future selling opportunities and deciding where to focus business efforts.
